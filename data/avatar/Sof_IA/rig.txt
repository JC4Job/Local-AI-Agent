# The bones of Sof_IA's avatar: what its animations (anims\) move. Without any, the whole model is one bone, root.
#
# bone = <name> <parent, or - for none> <pivot x y z, or auto> : <parts>
#
# A bone turns and scales its parts about its pivot, and the bones under it follow. A parent comes before its
# children. Parts are the .obj's object names: Name.L / Name.R is the one on the character's own left (+x) or
# right, Name#2 the second object of that name in the file, and * every part no bone takes. A part goes to the
# first bone that names it; auto puts the pivot in the middle of the bone's parts. Right-click the avatar >
# Model parts (or "avatar_render --agent Sof_IA --parts") lists the parts by these names.
#
# Format: one "key = value" per line. Lines starting with # are comments. Numbers use a decimal point.
