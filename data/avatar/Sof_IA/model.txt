# The window reads this folder again within a second of a change, so you can edit it with the window open.

# Format: one "key = value" per line. Lines starting with # are comments. Numbers use a decimal point.

# A Wavefront .obj, relative to the program's folder (or a full path). Its .mtl gives the colors (Kd, Ks, Ns).
# Its objects ("o" lines) are the parts the bones of rig.txt move; normals are worked out smooth, per part.
model = data/avatar/Sof_IA/Sofia.obj

# Which of the file's axes is up, and which one its front faces: x, y, z, -x, -y or -z. Left out: y and z, the

# Sofia.obj is already y up, front z: nothing to set.
